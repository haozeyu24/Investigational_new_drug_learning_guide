## Interpretation limits

The figure displays percentages by phase, not absolute trial counts or a count of trials relocated from the United States. Its patterns differ across phases. The footer's August 2025 date and the treatment of the 2025 point need to stay visible; the slide does not explain the full counting methodology. The text makes no causal claim that IND knowledge explains country-share trends.

The recommendation to understand program needs, design useful experiments, and coordinate across disciplines is the guide's educational position. It is not presented as an FDA legal requirement for individual early R&D scientists.
