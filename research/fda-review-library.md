# FDA review library — verification notes

Checked 16 September 2026. PDF page numbers are one-based file pages. The website list is maintained in `dist/fda-review-library.html`.

Inclusion: identifiable earlier IND-review material, substantive retrospective IND history, or original FDA correspondence. Merely naming an IND is insufficient. This audit is a selected search, not an exhaustive database.

## Ivacaftor — Earlier IND review

Appendix 1 preserves the 2006 pharmacology/toxicology review of the initial IND, subsequent responses, and an amendment. It evaluates safety pharmacology, ADME, repeat-dose toxicology, and the proposed clinical dose escalation.

This is one discipline’s review, not the complete IND. Other appendices cover later development.

- [Initial IND review · PDF pp. 79–122](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2012/203188Orig1s000PharmR.pdf#page=79)
- [Clinical-dose assessment · PDF p. 122](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2012/203188Orig1s000PharmR.pdf#page=122)

## Osimertinib — IND-review extract

An appendix explicitly reproduces material from the IND review: one-month rat and dog toxicity studies, recovery assessments, and toxicokinetics.

The extract begins at the Appendix/Attachments heading on PDF p. 134. Earlier text on that page belongs to the later NDA assessment. The full initial IND is not reproduced.

- [IND-review extract · PDF pp. 134–150](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2015/208065Orig1s000PharmR.pdf#page=134)
- [Rat toxicokinetics · PDF p. 140](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2015/208065Orig1s000PharmR.pdf#page=140)

## Suvorexant — Later summary of IND work

The review describes the initial one-month rat/dog studies and concerns in the June 2008 May Proceed letter about differences between nonclinical and clinical formulations. It traces subsequent formulation discussions and additional toxicology work.

Some formulation details are redacted. This is a retrospective account; do not infer the hidden excipient or apply the later study battery to every initial IND.

- [Formulation and IND history · PDF p. 34](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2014/204569Orig1s000PharmR.pdf#page=34)
- [Earlier pharmacology summarized · PDF p. 66](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2014/204569Orig1s000PharmR.pdf#page=66)

## Tofacitinib — IND-review extract

Table 13 explicitly credits a June 24, 2005 IND nonclinical review for the receptor/ion-channel screening information. The regulatory history separately describes how nonclinical questions evolved through later meetings and NDA review.

The reproduced item is a table, not an entire IND review. The NDA-era exposure comparisons and later reproductive-study issues should not be assigned to the initial IND.

- [IND-derived Table 13 · PDF p. 505](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2012/203214Orig1s000PharmR.pdf#page=505)

## Venetoclax — Later summary of IND work

The clinical review dates IND opening to 2010, a partial clinical hold to December 2012, and its removal to May 2013. It describes how tumor lysis events prompted changes to dose ramp-up, risk assessment, and prophylaxis.

These are clinical-development events after initial human dosing. They are not the original nonclinical package or a current dosing guide.

- [IND and hold history · PDF p. 28](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2016/208573Orig1s000MedR.pdf#page=28)
- [Phase 1 dosing changes · PDF p. 64](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2016/208573Orig1s000MedR.pdf#page=64)
- [Tumor lysis assessment · PDF pp. 96–99](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2016/208573Orig1s000MedR.pdf#page=96)

## Sotorasib — FDA meeting minutes

The package includes Type B pre-NDA meeting minutes under IND 145628, with questions and FDA responses on nonclinical sufficiency, clinical evidence, and clinical pharmacology. Its history identifies the 2018 first-in-human submission under IND 139023.

This is a late-development meeting, not a first-in-human IND review. The two IND numbers refer to different records within the program.

- [Meeting identity and history · PDF pp. 12–13](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2021/214665Orig1s000AdminCorres.pdf#page=12)
- [Questions and FDA responses · PDF pp. 17–20](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2021/214665Orig1s000AdminCorres.pdf#page=17)
