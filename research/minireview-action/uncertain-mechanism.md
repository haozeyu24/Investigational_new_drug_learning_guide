# Connecting drug action to possible benefit when the mechanism is incomplete

Seven program cases were manually read in nine original FDA review PDFs. Six are substantive development cases; trofinetide is a narrower supporting counterexample. These public documents are predominantly **NDA reviews that recount IND events**, not full contemporaneous initial IND submissions. Every case in the JSON separates the event stage from the document stage and separates FDA observations from our teaching inference. Page links use **PDF page numbers**, which can differ greatly from printed page numbers after redactions or bundled reviews.

The useful distinction is between **what the drug does in an experiment**, **why that effect might matter in a disease**, and **whether it improves an outcome in a defined population**. These records do not make a complete molecular explanation or genetic selection the default entry condition. They show investigators testing narrower claims, revising explanations, and sometimes learning that a proposed evidence bridge does not hold.

## 1. Lumateperone: target engagement is a question, not a mechanism certificate

**Initial IND, with later IND and NDA comparison.** The 2007 IND opened with an open-label receptor-occupancy study that FDA allowed to proceed. In 2014 FDA suggested integrating occupancy with dose-response for phase 3 dose selection. [FDA history, PDF 34](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2019/209500Orig1s000MultidisciplineR.pdf#page=34).

At NDA review, FDA challenged the claim that lumateperone uniquely modulated serotonin, dopamine, and glutamate. In vitro antagonist activity did not settle the proposed presynaptic partial-agonist story; SERT binding had not been functionally characterized. [Pharmacology, PDF 46–49](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2019/209500Orig1s000MultidisciplineR.pdf#page=46).

**Teaching inference:** a receptor-occupancy experiment can advance a program while leaving mechanism and comparative advantage unresolved. Ask which causal link an assay tests. **Caveat:** the later mechanistic dispute must not be retroactively attributed to the 2007 package.

## 2. Brexanolone: the first explanation can weaken while the clinical test remains informative

**Initial indication-specific IND.** FDA allowed a phase 2a open-label proof-of-concept study in adult women with severe PPD to proceed in 2014. [IND history, PDF 27–28](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2019/211371Orig1s000MultidisciplineR.pdf#page=27).

The NDA assessment unusually states that the allopregnanolone-replacement hypothesis influenced FDA's decision not to require non-PPD MDD studies. It also notes that hormone levels did not predict PPD; the benefit mechanism therefore remained unknown. Receptor-current potentiation still supported GABAA modulation. [Development rationale and limitation, PDF 19](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2019/211371Orig1s000MultidisciplineR.pdf#page=19); [functional pharmacology, PDF 35](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2019/211371Orig1s000MultidisciplineR.pdf#page=35).

**Teaching inference:** define what would survive rejection of the favored causal story. **Caveat:** this is not evidence for selecting patients by low allopregnanolone, and the initial PPD IND was not necessarily first human exposure.

## 3. Zuranolone: shared pharmacology does not make two populations interchangeable

**IND pharmacology plus later IND decisions.** FDA's section explicitly summarizing studies submitted with the IND reports receptor binding and current potentiation, while retaining uncertainty about PPD benefit mechanism. [PDF 154, printed 200](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2023/217369Orig2s000IntegratedR.pdf#page=154).

Development discussions initially entertained one positive PPD trial supported by two positive MDD trials. Later FDA requested another PPD study, and the September 2021 pre-NDA discussion cited potential differences in drug response between the populations. [PDF 150–153](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2023/217369Orig2s000IntegratedR.pdf#page=150).

**Teaching inference:** specify the evidence needed before borrowing support across related syndromes. The biological rationale may travel farther than the demonstrated treatment effect. **Caveat:** much of the MDD review is redacted; this does not reconstruct its complete-response rationale. “Submitted with the IND” does not establish that every study was in the initial 2016 submission.

## 4. Auvelity: a useful causal question may concern the combination, not a single molecular target

**Later IND/EOP2.** In April 2019 FDA agreed that dextromethorphan alone at the proposed combination dose would be metabolized too rapidly for direct evaluation. Conditional on successful studies, earlier comparison with bupropion could support component contribution, allowing a placebo-controlled phase 3 trial. [PDF 31–32](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2023/215430Orig1s000MultidisciplineR.pdf#page=31).

The NDA review identifies known receptor pharmacology and CYP2D6 inhibition but says the exact antidepressant mechanism remains unknown. Later deliberations distinguish contribution to a therapeutically relevant outcome from the overall efficacy claim. [PDF 69](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2023/215430Orig1s000MultidisciplineR.pdf#page=69); [PDF 191–192](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2023/215430Orig1s000MultidisciplineR.pdf#page=191).

**Teaching inference:** ask what each component adds at the exposure the combination actually produces. **Caveat:** the EOP2 agreement was conditional; it was neither an initial IND determination nor a guarantee of approval.

## 5. Pimavanserin: start with the clinical tradeoff that needs to change

**Later IND, with NDA pharmacology.** Parkinson disease psychosis presented a concrete problem: some antipsychotic treatment worsened motor function. Selective serotonergic functional activity and animal findings supported a plausible alternative, while the clinical reviewer retained uncertainty about psychosis mechanisms. [Clinical context, PDF 21–22](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2016/207318Orig1s000MedR.pdf#page=21); [pharmacology, PDF 47–50](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2016/207318Orig1s000PharmR.pdf#page=47).

The IND program revised trial design and endpoints after high placebo response, eventually obtaining a filing agreement based on a strongly positive study and supporting evidence. [FDA meeting history, PDF 23–25](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2016/207318Orig1s000MedR.pdf#page=23).

**Teaching inference:** an interpretable test of an important symptom can be more immediately useful than resolving the entire disease mechanism. **Caveat:** model effects are NDA-stage evidence; one repeated-dose rodent effect developed tolerance. The individual clinical review's approval recommendation is not treated here as the final agency outcome.

## 6. Dimethyl fumarate: a pathway association is not a complete explanation

**NDA mechanism synthesis, linked to separate pre-IND/initial IND history.** FDA reviewed immunomodulatory and EAE findings across several pathways. The sponsor emphasized Nrf2, but one macrophage response did not require it, and measured gene responses varied across tissues. [PDF 41–42](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2013/204063Orig1s000PharmR.pdf#page=41).

The clinical review recounts pre-IND discussion of interpretable trial designs and an initial hold for inadequate prior human safety information, lifted after a response. [PDF 188, printed 14](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2013/204063Orig1s000MedR.pdf#page=188).

**Teaching inference:** convergent functional evidence can justify a disease hypothesis without making a favored pathway necessary or sufficient. **Caveat:** no retrieved passage establishes that FDA accepted Nrf2 as the initial IND efficacy mechanism. The clinical and pharmacology reviews give different 2006 IND-submission dates; the JSON retains that discrepancy.

## Supporting contrast: trofinetide makes concentration relevance explicit

**Later IND abuse assessment, recounted at NDA.** FDA noted that a neuroprotective effect was hypothesized at nanomolar concentrations, whereas proposed glutamate-receptor binding appeared only at micromolar concentrations. Subsequent functional NMDA assays were negative. The same review recounts an IND request for additional evidence before accepting the sponsor's plan without dedicated abuse studies. [PDF 22–23](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2023/217026Orig1s000OtherR.pdf#page=22).

**Teaching inference:** an explanation must work at relevant concentrations; rejecting it does not answer every separate development question. **Caveat:** this source does not establish the full Rett disease-benefit bridge, and it inconsistently cites IND 140184 and 114319. Use it as a supporting counterexample, not a complete initial IND case.

## Suggested lesson structure

1. **Make a bounded prediction.** Define the population's problem and the outcome the drug might change. Explain whether the rationale comes from human biology, functional assays, disease models, prior clinical observations, or a combination.
2. **Mark the uncertain link.** Distinguish binding from functional action, engagement from benefit, pathway association from necessity, and shared symptoms from transferable treatment response. Record a competing explanation and a result that would change the plan.
3. **Choose the next informative test.** Occupancy may inform dose; a component comparison may test causal contribution; a population-specific trial may test whether an extrapolation survives. The output is a short claim–evidence–uncertainty–next-test argument, not a requirement to solve all biology before human study.

The strongest three anchor stories for a compact lesson are **lumateperone, brexanolone, and Auvelity**. Zuranolone makes the best extension for population choice. DMF broadens beyond a named receptor; pimavanserin broadens the clinical problem. Trofinetide is useful only as a focused challenge to an overconfident mechanistic claim.
