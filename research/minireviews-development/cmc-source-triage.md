# CMC mini-review source audit

Prepared 17 September 2026. This is a bounded, purposive evidence review, not a systematic estimate of all CMC issues in FDA development programs. The three reviews contain eleven cases each, drawn from 23 distinct development programs; overlapping cases answer different lesson questions. Glycopyrrolate inhalation and the later injectable-syringe program are separate development programs, not two different active substances.

The first pass re-read critical passages in previously curated CMC records and inspected focused FDA-source additions: ACER-001, CyclASol and glycopyrrolate injection NDA 214919. The fresh frozen screen subsequently contained 7,979 PDF documents, representing 7,933 source hashes and 467,510 pages, with 77,998 pages flagged for low text. Topic retrieval returned 1,570 material documents, 1,301 controls documents and 971 stability documents. These are overlapping keyword/relevance queues, not counts of verified substantive cases.

Seven unique fresh-queue sources were manually inspected. Four supplied additional cases: vamorolone, Sesquient, Cytalux and Xaciato. Bortezomib, Zimhi and Soaanz contained substantive passages but were not added because their strongest points overlapped better-developed selected examples. None was classified as irrelevant merely because it was not selected. Deutetrabenazine was a focused-search lead whose relevant scanned passage was not reverified; it is explicitly excluded from the evidence base. The JSON audit records URLs, pages read, queue positions, selection reasons and limits.

Verification used FDA PDF text already cached by the corpus process, with visual checks for scanned or defective text where critical: Pennsaid, Atrovent, osimertinib, teplizumab, dupilumab, Gvoke, glycopyrrolate composition tables and Fiasp's missing-report discussion. One-based PDF pages are used throughout. The year in an FDA package URL can differ from the enclosed meeting or review date. No source is called initial IND merely because it includes an IND number or a pre-IND reference.

Three corrections are particularly important for reuse:

- Ivacaftor's visible vehicle descriptions surround redacted material identifiers. They do not disclose a complete clinical recipe or prove a final commercial-product bridge.
- Glycopyrrolate's 2011 memo and 2017 review describe benzoic-acid genotoxicity differently. The historical wording is not propagated as current hazard classification or numerical guidance.
- Glycopyrrolate syringe filing grounds, additional filing comments, and fosphenytoin's expressly non-approvability monograph comment must not be collapsed into one cause of regulatory failure.

Public manufacturing detail remains substantially redacted. The controls review therefore supports reasoning about process outputs, method capability, impurities, investigations and change continuity; it cannot reproduce complete process ranges or commercial control strategies. The stability review has one unusually direct initial IND anchor and several strong later-development comparators. This asymmetry is reported rather than filled with inferred early evidence.

No website, catalog, built lesson, prior mapping or bulk-index file was edited for these reviews.
