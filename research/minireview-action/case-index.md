## Reading the records

An IND application is the sponsor’s submission. An FDA review, assessment or meeting record tells us what the agency evaluated or discussed; it may summarize rather than reproduce that submission. A later NDA/BLA review may retain earlier IND material, add new evidence, or reinterpret an earlier hypothesis. Source-stage labels apply to the particular passage, not automatically to the entire PDF.

Full case records retain scientific limitations, incomplete information, redactions and detected inconsistencies. The narrative mini-review deliberately avoids disputed numerical details.
